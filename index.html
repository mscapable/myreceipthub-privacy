MyReceiptHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

MyReceiptHub ("MyReceiptHub", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information MyReceiptHub collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you when using the MyReceiptHub app.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MyReceiptHub, the app may collect or store:

  • Receipt and financial information: Information contained in receipts, including purchase amounts, merchant information, dates, categories, and other purchase details that you choose to save.
  • Photos and videos: Images of receipts or other documents that you choose to capture, scan, import, or store in the app.
  • Other user content: Information you enter or create within the app, such as notes, tags, folder information, receipt details, and other content associated with your receipts.

2. How We Collect Information

Information is collected when you voluntarily use MyReceiptHub, including when you scan or import a receipt, enter receipt information, add notes or other content, organise receipts, or use other app features.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide and operate MyReceiptHub, including to:

  • Store and organise your receipts.
  • Display and search your receipt information.
  • Process receipt images and information for app functionality.
  • Provide folders, reports, spending insights, and other features you choose to use.
  • Synchronise your information across your supported Apple devices.
  • Provide premium subscription features.
  • Respond to support or privacy enquiries.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability and security of the app.

4. iCloud and CloudKit

MyReceiptHub uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to store and synchronise your receipt information and related content between supported devices.

Your MyReceiptHub data stored through CloudKit is associated with your Apple/iCloud account and is intended to remain private to you. MyReceiptHub does not use your receipt data for advertising or behavioural tracking.

Your use of iCloud and CloudKit is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

MyReceiptHub offers optional premium subscription features. Subscriptions and payments are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

MyReceiptHub does not collect or store your credit card, debit card, or other payment card details. Payment information is handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's privacy policies and terms.

6. Tracking and Advertising

MyReceiptHub does not sell your personal information and does not use your information for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking.

We do not use receipt information or your stored receipt content to build advertising profiles about you.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Your information may be processed through services provided by Apple where necessary to operate MyReceiptHub, including iCloud, CloudKit, the App Store, and StoreKit.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with a legal obligation, enforce our terms, protect the security of the service, or protect the rights, safety, or property of users or others.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your MyReceiptHub information for as long as it is needed to provide the app's functionality or until you delete it.

You can delete receipts and other content through the app's available deletion features. Content placed in the app's Recently Deleted or Bin area may remain there for a limited period before permanent deletion.

If you want to request deletion of your MyReceiptHub data or have a privacy-related question, contact us at sorters44ambush@icloud.com .

9. Children's Privacy

MyReceiptHub is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by MyReceiptHub. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to MyReceiptHub, applicable laws, or our privacy practices. When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.
